How Tells decides (design notes)
Asks + pressure = riskEach signal is either an ask (money, codes, a link) or pressure (deadlines, threats, authority, secrecy). Pressure alone is capped at 30, because "ASAP" and "urgent" are normal in real life.
Explain, don't just scoreEvery point is tied to a flag, every flag shows the exact words that triggered it, and the message is highlighted in place. A number nobody can question is a number nobody can learn from.
Known patterns get a floorInvoice redirects, gift card requests, code theft, delivery texts and "wrong number" openers are recognized as patterns, so a scam with no ask yet (like a pig butchering opener) still gets flagged.
Tells is an educational triage aid, not a guarantee. When in doubt, contact the sender through a channel you already trust. Report scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
